## New Funds

Acquired for innovative ideas!

The NUS has been working with ISoN to incorporate new incentives for nursing research projects, events, and committees. Through this new fund, the NUS can provide financial support for **your** innovative ideas and bring them to life! If you have any new ideas or propositions or are curious about this fund, reach out to our NUS Director of Financial Affairs!

Procedures and confirmations are still in progress as this fund is new. We are thankful for your understanding.
