Hello Everyone!

The Equity Committee is reaching out and excited to kick off the year with your input!

To make sure we’re organizing events, activities, and equity-related changes that genuinely interest you, we’d love to hear your ideas. We want to plan meaningful, enjoyable, and inclusive events for everyone.

Please take a moment to fill out the form below with your suggestions for equity-focused activities and changes you’d like to see at the ISoN. Whether it’s a culture night, workshops, fundraisers, or any other idea you have in mind – we’re open to it all! Our goal is to create events that you’ll be excited to join, so your feedback would be incredibly helpful.

Thank you for helping us shape a more engaging and inclusive community! Our Instagram will be linked below!

Best,

The Equity Committee.
