The NUS Constitution is a document that outlines the fundamental principles and established legislation according to which the NUS council is governed.

The NUS' Constitution and its by-laws are enforced by the NUS' President.

Students are welcome to consult the NUS Constitution and By-Laws which are available below in PDF format.

- NUS Constitution
- NUS Role and Governance By-Laws
- NUS Financial By-Laws
- NUS General Electoral By-Laws
- NUS Committees & External Groups By-Laws
- SSMU-NUS Memorandum of Agreement

## NUS Meeting Minutes

The NUS Council holds frequent meetings every semester that follow an agenda. According to the Constitution, any nursing student is allowed to attend the NUS Council meetings and have access to the meeting minutes/agenda.

If you wish to attend a NUS council meeting, please contact [nus.nursing@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:nus.nursing@mail.mcgill.ca)
