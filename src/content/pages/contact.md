**Office Hours:**

Office hours vary as NUS members will not always be in the office. However, if you wish to speak to a council member feel free to contact them directly or email [nus.nursing@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:nus.nursing@mail.mcgill.ca) to schedule a meeting. You can view the email for most NUS members below.

If you **are a McGill Entity** looking to message the NUS for an invite or to share an event, please message the **NUS President at [president.nus@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:president.nus@mail.mcgill.ca).**

If you are **not a McGill Entity**, e.g., companies/organizations not involved with McGill, wishing to contact the NUS, please message the **NUS Director of External Affairs at [external.nus@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:external.nus@mail.mcgill.ca).**

For other **opportunities**, e.g., **job offerings**, fairs, etc., please contact the **NUS Opportunities Director at [opportunities.nus@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:opportunities.nus@mail.mcgill.ca).**

If you are unsure, please use the **form submission box below**!

Thank you!
