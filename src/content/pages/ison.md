The McGill Ingram School of Nursing (ISoN), a professional School within the Faculty of Medicine, has demonstrated leadership in nursing education since 1920.

For more information about the ISoN at McGill, please refer to their [official website](https://www.mcgill.ca/nursing/).

As presented on the ISoN official website, a Faculty and [Student Handbook](https://www.mcgill.ca/nursing/students/student-handbooks) was developed to centralize information and to provide guidance regarding academic matters, processes and procedures. Please refer to the handbook attached below. For questions regarding academic matters, please contact our VPs Academic.

[BScN Academic Affairs Coordinator](mailto:bscn.nus@mail.mcgill.ca)

[BNI Academic Affairs Coordinator](mailto:bni.nus@mail.mcgill.ca)
