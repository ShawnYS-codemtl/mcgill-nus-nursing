NUS Winter Elections 2026 are here!❄️🗳️

The NUS is a great way to be involved in the McGill nursing community. Different positions are available to engage and connect with other nursing students.

Interested in applying? Make sure to check your inbox for the email sent by [nus.nursing@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:nus.nursing@mail.mcgill.ca) for all the details on the application process, open positions, and timeline.

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact either [andrea.lian2@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:andrea.lian2@mail.mcgill.ca) or [president.nus@mail.mcgill.ca](mailto:president.nus@mail.mcgill.ca)
